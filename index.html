Report a problem or ask a question using our support tracker. Include your device, iOS version, and what happened. Please keep passwords, phone numbers and other private information out of public reports.

Sound and output beeps

Open Settings using the gear button. Sound controls all audio; Output sounds controls production beeps separately. You can also adjust haptics, Reduced Motion and Battery Saver.

Progress and resetting

Your progress is saved locally on your device. Quantum rebuilds the machine with bonuses while keeping permanent progress. Settings → Reset Progress clears the game after confirmation. Removing the app may remove its local save.

Offline progress

After at least a minute away, the machine can collect half its normal unassisted output for up to two hours. Return and collect your earnings once.

Interacting with the machine

Touch the components to help production. Hold an Upgrade button to upgrade repeatedly. Double-tap the total output rate to open Electrician and see how the numbers are calculated.
