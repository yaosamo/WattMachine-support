Privacy

The Machine for iOS · Updated November 6, 2026

The Machine does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. It has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics service or in-app purchases.

Local game data

Progress, preferences and the time you last played are stored in the app’s local save on your device. These support saving the game and calculating offline progress. The game does not send this information to us. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Device features

The game plays sounds and can use haptics. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

External links and support

Links to CreativeClub and support open external services, which follow their own privacy policies. If you choose to contact us, the information you submit is used to answer your request. Our public issue tracker is visible to others; do not include private information in a report.

Deleting progress

Use Start over in Settings to clear game progress. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to any device backup you maintain.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact support. No game data is automatically attached to your message.
